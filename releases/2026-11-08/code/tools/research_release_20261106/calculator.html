TBI post-ICU research calculator

Adults with acute traumatic brain injury surviving transfer out of their index ICU episode. Landmark t0: ICU episode transfer/discharge alive. Outcome: all-cause mortality within 90 and 180 days after t0. Scale: native model probability (not recalibrated).

Research only. No independent external validation. Do not use to restrict, withdraw, or limit treatment. No individual risk intervals or treatment thresholds are provided.

Model: corrected-cohort-candidate10-e41b9066ec45. Primary: fixed 10 fields; 14-field model is backup only. All events must occur before t0 and be available by t0; this tool cannot verify EHR timestamps.

Blank is permitted only where explicitly marked unavailable (and both WPR fields together). Missingness follows the original saved training-local preprocessing, not a normal-value substitution. 10^9/L and K/uL are numerically equivalent, but API unit labels must match their specific fields exactly.
